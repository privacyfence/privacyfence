1. Restrict, grant, revoke

about 15 min

This confirms Peek's email and access check with a real second person.

  1. Ask your AI client to publish a small HTML page to Peek, without saying who can open it.The approval card shows Visibility: restricted. Approve it. The result says "visibility": "restricted".
  2. Send the page link to your colleague and ask them to open it while signed in to Peek.They are refused: the page is restricted and they have no grant yet.
  3. Ask the assistant to give your colleague access, by their email.The card is titled Grant Access to Peek Page and says Peek emails them the link. Approve it. The result has "notified": true when Peek has email set up.
  4. Your colleague checks their inbox and opens the page again.The email from Peek arrives (if your Peek server has SMTP configured) and the page opens.
  5. Ask the assistant who can open the page.A review card Read Peek Page Access lists your colleague's email. Approve it.
  6. Ask the assistant to remove their access, then ask your colleague to reload the page.The Revoke Access to Peek Page card appears even if you have a Peek page rule. After you approve, they are refused again.
  7. Ask the assistant to delete the test page.
