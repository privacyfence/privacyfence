Grist connector: the steps only you can do

Do the two Before implementation tasks before you run /implement; the QA-recording phase needs them. Do the After implementation checks once the feature PR is up, and tick them in the PR. Your ticks here are saved in this browser only.

Part 1

Before implementation

1. Create a Grist QA account, seed document and OAuth app

mb1-grist-qa-accountabout 20 min

Phase p10 records live fixtures from this document, signed in through this app. Use an account that holds nothing real.

The client secret is the app's password. Never paste it into a chat, the plan, a PR, an issue or a GitHub secret. Its only other copy is the runner's QA bundle in task 2 (ADR 0019).

Done when: the seed and contrast documents exist, the "PrivacyFence QA" app has the redirect URI http://localhost:53685/callback and exactly the four permissions, its client id and secret are in your password manager, and you know which address serves the discovery document.

2. Put the Grist app on the QA runner and sign it in once

mb2-runner-qa-stateabout 15 min

Phase p10 dispatches qa-record-fixture.yml, which copies ~/privacyfence/ from the runner (labelled privacyfence-test) into its checkout (connector-qa.md, Persistent QA state). The script in step 4 also tries Grist's real sign-in once, before any code is written.

Done when: on the runner, ~/privacyfence/org/org_config.json has a grist section, ~/privacyfence/credentials/grist_token.json exists with mode 600 and the keys server_url, access_token, refresh_token and expires_at, and qa_environment.yaml has a grist section with doc_id and table_id. Tell the /implement session that both before-steps are done, and whether the script reported "basic" or "post".

Part 2

After implementation

3. Sign in to Grist from Settings and drive every Grist tool from an AI client

ma1-local-mode-checkabout 30 min

Proves the real OAuth sign-in, what the approval cards show and that real writes land. Unit tests and the recorder cannot show any of this.

Report back: tick the item in the PR and paste one line, for example ma1 local mode: PASS (sign-in, listing allowed/refused, review card, 4 write cards, rule, revoke), or FAIL at step N: what you saw.

4. Sign in to Grist on /connect in an org-mode test deployment

ma2-org-mode-checkabout 20 min · only if you run one

Proves the bundle's grist section, the org redirect and the per-person sign-in. No CI job runs an org deployment against Grist.

Report back: tick the item in the PR and paste ma2 org mode: PASS, FAIL at step N: what you saw, or ma2: skipped, no org-mode test deployment.
