# Miro setup

This guide covers connecting Miro to PrivacyFence: an assistant can list your boards and tags, read items after your review, and add or change items with your approval, and nothing is ever deleted.

## Register the PrivacyFence app

This section is for administrators: it covers creating the PrivacyFence app in a Miro Developer team with expiring tokens, the `boards:read` and `boards:write` permissions and the redirect URIs.

## Values

This section lists the client id and client secret and the `build_org_bundle.py` option that takes each one.

## Build and distribute the bundle

This section covers putting the Miro app into the organization config bundle and handing it to people.

## Connect

This section covers signing in with **Authenticate…** in Settings or **Connect** on an organization server; the connection covers one Miro team.

## What the assistant can do

This section lists the Miro tools, the gate each one has, and how batches, frame positions and content are handled.

## Auto-accept rules

This section covers the per-board `miro.board` rule and where it is set.

## Troubleshooting

This section lists the error messages a person can meet when connecting or using Miro, and what to do about each.
