What the relay is

The relay is a free, non-commercial service at oauth.privacyfence.eu. When a PrivacyFence server signs a person in to a service with one of PrivacyFence's built-in apps (or with the built-in Google sign-in), the provider can send the sign-in redirect only to one fixed address. The relay is that address. It forwards the redirect to the PrivacyFence server that started the sign-in. It is operated by the publisher named in the imprint.
