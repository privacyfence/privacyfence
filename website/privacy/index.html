The pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which acts as a content delivery network and protects the site against attacks. To deliver a page, both receive the technical data every web request carries: your IP address, the requested address, the time, and your browser's request headers (such as its user agent and language). Legal basis: legitimate interest in delivering a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Both companies may process data in the United States; both are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Sign-in relay (oauth.privacyfence.eu)

PrivacyFence ships OAuth apps of its own for Google, Slack, Salesforce, Atlassian and Miro. A provider sends the sign-in redirect only to a fixed address, so the redirect for these apps goes to oauth.privacyfence.eu, a small service run on Cloudflare (a Worker and a Durable Object). The relay forwards the redirect to the PrivacyFence server that started the sign-in, and only to a server that proved in advance that it controls its own host name; a single-user install on its own computer, which uses a local address, needs no registration. It is used only by organization servers and single-user installs that use the built-in apps. An organization's own apps never touch it, and desktop (local) installs do not use it.

  • The redirect it forwards carries a one-time authorization code and the server's host name. It never sees a token, mail or file content. The relay processes the IP address of the request in transit.
  • Servers using the built-in Google sign-in also send their sign-in redirects through the relay. The relay sees a one-time code and the signing-in account's Google Workspace domain (hd), checks it against the sign-in domains the server proved in DNS, and stores those domains with the server's registration.
  • It stores each registered server's host name, its registration and renewal dates and its proven sign-in domains. This list is visible only to the PrivacyFence maintainers.
  • To limit abuse it also keeps the requesting IP address next to each registration attempt for one hour, and a per-address count of new servers for the current day (UTC). Both are deleted automatically.
  • It writes no logs.
  • When a new server registers, or a server changes its sign-in domains, it emails the maintainers the host name, the IP address and the country of the request. The email is sent through PrivacyFence's Google Workspace mail, where Google is the processor, and is kept in that mailbox for up to 12 months.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating and securing the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is the processor, as for the download service. The terms for the relay are at Relay terms.

Google user data in the PrivacyFence app

This section covers the Google connectors of PrivacyFence's built-in Google app.

What it accesses

Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks and Apps Script, only after the person signs in and consents. Each scope is used for what the connector pages describe.

Where it goes

  • Tokens and data stay on the person's computer or their organization's server, never on PrivacyFence's.
  • Content reaches the AI assistant the person connected only when they approve it on a card, or when a rule they set allows it, after the local personal-data check.
  • The sign-in relay sees a one-time sign-in code, never mail or file content.

What never happens

No ads, no sale of data, no human reading, and no use to train AI models by PrivacyFence. What the AI assistant does with content it is given is governed by that assistant's own terms.

Revoking access

Disconnect Google on the /connect page of your PrivacyFence, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

PrivacyFence's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to see how many people visit, which pages they read and where they came from. Until you accept, the Google Analytics code is not loaded at all and no request goes to Google.

Once you accept, Google Analytics collects:

  • the pages you view, the page that referred you, and the time of your visit;
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language;
  • your approximate location (country and city), which Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses;
  • interactions on the page: scrolling, clicks on links to other sites, file downloads, and which platform's download button you click;
  • the section of the site a page belongs to (for example, download or documentation).

Google Ireland Limited processes the data on our behalf. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States, which is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from its business products.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive for the cookies). You can withdraw it at any time: choose Cookie settings at the bottom of any page and click Decline. That stops Google Analytics on this site and deletes its cookies. Withdrawing does not affect what was collected before.
