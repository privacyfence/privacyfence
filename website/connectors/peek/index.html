Connector · Peek

Your shared HTML pages for your AI assistant, changed only with your approval.

Peek is a self-hosted server for sharing HTML pages and collecting comments. PrivacyFence connects an AI assistant to your Peek account: it lists your pages straight away, reads comments and visit counts after you have reviewed them, and publishes, changes who can open a page, comments or deletes only with your approval.

What the assistant can do

List, read after review, change after approval.

Every Peek tool has a gate fixed in code. The Tools reference lists each one.

Without a card

Your page list and a page's versions: names, links, sizes and who can open it. The call is still audited.

Reviewed before release

The comments reviewers left on a page, how often it was opened, and who has access. Visit counts never include IP addresses.

Needs your approval

Publishing a page or a new version, changing who can open a page, granting or removing access, commenting on a page, renaming it and deleting it.

Set it up

Sign in with Peek.

  1. Have a Peek server you can sign in to.
  2. In PrivacyFence's Settings page, choose Peek, enter the server address and approve the code in the browser that opens. You connect with Peek's own sign-in.
  3. The Peek tools appear to the assistant.
