Claude's built-in connectors or PrivacyFence?

Claude has its own connectors for Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Slack, Jira, Confluence and Salesforce, and they are the quickest way to start. PrivacyFence connects Claude to the same services through a checkpoint you run yourself. The difference is where that checkpoint sits, what you see before data is released, and who keeps the record.

Checked against Anthropic's and the partners' documentation on 29 September 2026. Connectors change often; the sources are at the end of this page.

Short answer

Pick by what you need to control.

Claude's connectors fit when

  • you want it working in minutes, on every device Claude runs on, with nothing to install or operate;
  • approving actions such as sending, sharing or deleting is enough, and reads can go straight into the conversation;
  • your data may be retrieved and stored through Anthropic's service under your agreement with Anthropic.

PrivacyFence fits when

  • you want to see the actual content of a sensitive read before Claude gets it, and deny it if needed;
  • you want personal data checked for, and whole categories withheld, before anything reaches the AI;
  • you want the connection and its credentials on your own computers, and a record of every decision you keep.

Using both

Turn off the built-in connector for the same service.

PrivacyFence and Claude's own connectors can sit side by side, but only one should reach each service. If the built-in Gmail connector stays on next to PrivacyFence's Gmail tools, Claude can read the mailbox either way, and the checkpoint only covers the calls that happen to go through it.

  • For services PrivacyFence governs, switch off Claude's connector. On Team and Enterprise plans an owner can do that for everyone.
  • For services it doesn't, keep using Claude's connectors as before.
